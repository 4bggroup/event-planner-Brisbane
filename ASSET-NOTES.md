# Event Studio — photography and font notes

This version has 16 different photographic masters assigned to 16 different roles: two hero images, one studio image, six service images and seven gallery images. The initial page renders 11 photo elements; every source differs. Switching services introduces a service-only image. The modal viewer intentionally opens the corresponding gallery original.

Nine images were newly created with the built-in image generation tool for this revision. Seven earlier generated masters remain assigned to a single role each. Images are illustrative concepts, not real completed client events. Masters and 640px variants use lossless WebP encoding; all native master dimensions are preserved.

## Typeface

The unmodified Poppins Regular, Medium and SemiBold fonts are self-hosted in dist/assets/fonts/ with their OFL licence. Source: the Google Fonts Poppins directory at https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/poppins. No system-font substitution is used ahead of Poppins.

A sculptural asymmetric installation of white orchids and soft blush roses frames the end of the aisle, elegant sage-green chairs arranged in two intimate rows, pale travertine floor, high white steel and glass architecture, lush green gardens beyond. No dining tables. No people. Bright clean daylight with accurate neutral colours, crisp fine detail in the chairs, petals and architecture, balanced contrast, f/8 deep focus. A completely new, carefully composed visual for an event planner website. No haze, glow, fog, grain, bokeh, artificial blur, text, logos or watermark. Natural believable photography, not a render. Highest native resolution, one tall photo.
