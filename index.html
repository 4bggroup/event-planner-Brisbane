Ask us anything ↗
Where do we even begin?+

Start with whatever you know: the type of occasion, an approximate date, a place you love or a feeling you want to create. We can talk through the rest together.

Can you help with styling only?+

Yes. Styling and design can be the starting point, whether you need help with a complete visual direction or a few carefully chosen details. Tell us what you have in mind.

Do you plan smaller celebrations?+

Intimate occasions deserve just as much care. Share your guest numbers, location and ideas so we can explore the right support for your gathering.

What if we've already started planning?+

That's completely fine. Let us know what's already organised and where you'd appreciate some help, from a creative consultation to coordination closer to the day.

How much does event planning cost?+

The scope depends on your occasion, guest numbers, location and the support you need. Share those details first so a tailored proposal can reflect the actual work involved.

How early should we get in touch?+

Get in touch once you have an occasion or a possible date in mind. You do not need a finished plan. Share what you know, and we can discuss the next steps and the support you may need.
